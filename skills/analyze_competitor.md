# Skill: analyze_competitor

## Purpose
Analyze a competitor's positioning, pricing, content, or strategy.
Find gaps and recommend a counter-strategy.

## Extract from input
- Competitor name and type (clinic / brand / agency)
- Pricing if mentioned or visible
- Content style and posting frequency
- Positioning statement or USP
- Audience they target
- Screenshot content (ads, posts, website, menu)

## Analysis framework
- Strengths: what they do well
- Weaknesses: gaps, blind spots, poor execution
- Positioning gap: what they OWN vs what's open
- Differentiation opportunity: where we can win
